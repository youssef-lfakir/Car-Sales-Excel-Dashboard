# 🚗 Executive Car Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This interactive Excel Dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of automotive sales data, evaluating revenue streams, unit volumes, customer purchasing power, and dealer distribution metrics. Designed with an executive **Dark Theme UI**, the dashboard enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions regarding inventory management, model prioritization, and sales channel optimization.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** **$267.14M** ($267,138,050) total generated sales volume.
- **Total Units Sold:** **13,226** vehicles delivered across all categories.
- **Average Vehicle Price:** **$28,090.25** per car sold.
- **Average Customer Income:** **$830,840.29**, indicating a strong high-net-worth client base.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Market Segmentation & Body Style Preferences
- **SUV & Passenger Lead:** SUVs (27%) and Passenger cars (28%) dominate total sales, together accounting for **55%** of all customer transactions.
- **Hardtop Niche:** Hardtop models represent the lowest volume share at **12%**, suggesting a specialized niche focus.

### 2. Transmission & Technical Specifications
- **Balanced Transmission Demand:** Automatic transmissions lead slightly at **53%**, compared to Manual at **47%**, indicating balanced preference depending on regional driving trends and vehicle classification.
- **Engine Configuration:** Overhead Camshaft configurations constitute the vast majority of sold engines compared to Double Overhead Camshaft variants.

### 3. Customer Preferences & Popularity
- **Color Dominance:** **Pale White** is by far the most popular vehicle color (~11k units), followed by **Black** (~8k units), while **Red** records the lowest volume (~5k units).

### 4. Dealer Performance & Distribution Channels
- High sales concentration is observed among top-performing dealers such as *Rabun Used Car Sales* and *Progressive Shippers*, outperforming regional averages in revenue generation.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Inventory Optimization:** Prioritize stock allocation for **Pale White** and **Black** SUVs/Passenger models to maximize turnover speed and minimize holding costs.
2. **Dealer Enablement:** Replicate the sales and marketing practices of top-tier dealers (*Rabun Used Car Sales*) across underperforming branches to normalize revenue distribution.
3. **Targeted Marketing:** Capitalize on the high average customer income ($830k+) by introducing upselling packages, premium trim upgrades, and extended warranties.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture & Excel Features
- **Pivot Tables & Calculated Fields:** Dynamic aggregation of raw dataset containing 13,000+ transaction records.
- **Advanced Visualization:** Custom-styled Donut Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts, and Metric Cards.
- **Dynamic Interactivity:** Multi-slicer controls for real-time filtering by **Company**, **Model**, and **Dealer Name**.
- **UX/UI Design:** Gridless Layout with high-contrast Dark Navy background (`#0B192C`) and clean visual card containers.
